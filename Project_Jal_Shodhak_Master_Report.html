National Mission Statement: To safeguard the nation's agrarian arteries and rural water security by deploying autonomous amphibious robotic harvesters that clear weed-choked irrigation canals, restore natural drainage to prevent seasonal floods, and convert noxious aquatic biomass into decentralized, high-calorific clean biofuel pellets for rural communities.

1 The National Crisis & Problem Statement

Millions of kilometers of irrigation canals, river tributaries, and municipal water channels across the nation suffer from severe biological and hydrological choke points that jeopardize agricultural output and public safety:

Core Threats to the Nation:
  • Canal Choking by Water Hyacinth (Eichhornia crassipes): Hyacinth weed doubles in biomass every 10–12 days, forming dense floating mats that reduce water velocity and canal throughput by up to 70%, causing downstream tail-end farms to dry up during critical sowing cycles.
  • Devastating Monsoon Flooding: Weed and silt choked drainage canals create massive backflow surges during heavy rains, causing earthen embankments to breach and triggering catastrophic rural and urban flash floods.
  • Ecological Suffocation: Dense canopy blocks sunlight and consumes dissolved oxygen, triggering mass fish mortality and turning vital water reservoirs into stagnant breeding grounds for disease vectors.
  • Failure of Existing Practices: Traditional diesel excavators (JCBs) operating from canal banks tear down fragile earthen bunds, burn ~14 L/hr of diesel, and dump rotting weed piles along roadsides that emit foul methane odors and toxic runoff.
🚨 Conventional Backhoe Dredging (Current Method)

Heavy machinery operating from banks. Destroys earthen bunds, burns expensive diesel (~$18/hr), leaves roots intact in the water, and creates foul roadside dumps.

💡 The Jal-Shodhak Solution (Our Innovation)

Ultralight amphibious catamaran entering the water directly. Solar-electric powered, cuts roots 0.8m deep, shreds and converts wet weed into solid biofuel pellets on-board.

3 Mechanical & Mechatronic Engineering Subsystems

A. Amphibious Twin-Catamaran Hull & Crawlers

Constructed from marine-grade corrosion-resistant Aluminum 5083-H116 and rotomolded HDPE floats. Features an ultra-shallow operational draft of 0.22 meters to glide over shallow weed beds and mud shoals.

  • Propulsion: Dual 48V steerable BLDC Kort-nozzle thrusters with weed-chopping hub blades to prevent fouling.
  • Bank Traversal: Retractable paddle-crawler tracks allow the drone to climb across canal bunds, locks, and weirs.
B. Submerged Harvester & Mulcher

Front articulated conveyor table equipped with dual reciprocating cutter bars engineered with tungsten-carbide serrated teeth. Submerges up to 0.9 m to cleanly slice root crowns.

  • Hexagonal Shredder: High-torque twin shafts running at 180 RPM shred dense vegetation into 15–20 mm uniform mulch.
  • Anti-Jam Torque Monitor: Dynamic load sensing automatically reverses cutters if nylon rope or scrap debris is detected.
C. Continuous Screw-Press Dewaterer

Fresh water hyacinth contains 92–95% water by weight. Jal-Shodhak uses a progressive tapered compression screw with a slotted wedge-wire cylindrical screen.

  • Moisture Drop: Reduces moisture from 94% down to under 35% in seconds.
  • Clean Effluent Return: Filtered, oxygenated water returns to the canal, replenishing local dissolved oxygen.
D. Radial Die Extrusion Pelletizer

The compressed biomass cake enters a rotary roller die extruder. Motor cooling jacket heat (85°C) liquefies the plant's natural lignin, bonding dense 8mm solid fuel pellets without chemical additives.

  • Energy Density: ~16.8 MJ/kg (competes directly with dry wood briquettes).
  • On-Board Bunker: 350 kg capacity with automated offloading auger for seamless canal-bank unloading.

7 Field Deployment SOP: Pre-Float & Software Boot

Follow this exact Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) before putting the autonomous drone in the water:

Phase 1: Site Survey & RTK Setup

Fly a low-altitude mapping drone or use Sentinel-2 satellite NDVI data to locate weed choke-points. Set up the RTK-GNSS base station tripod on a firm canal embankment point. Confirm LoRa telemetry differential lock (±2cm accuracy).

Phase 2: Water Entry Slipway Setup

Back the custom low-bed transport trailer into the canal ramp or slipway. Release the electric winch strap. The drone floats off into the water, engages reverse Kort thrusters, and holds station via dynamic positioning.

8 Autonomous Harvesting & Biomass Offloading Operations

Step A: In-Situ Cutting & Mulching

The drone engages the submerged reciprocating cutter blades, slicing through root crowns. The intake conveyor continuously sweeps the biomass into the counter-rotating shredder, mulching 120 kg of wet hyacinth per minute into uniform 15mm pulp.

Step B: Continuous Dewatering & Pelletizing

The progressive tapered screw press squeezes out 85% of plant water, returning clear aerated liquid to the stream. The dried fibrous cake is extruded through the heated radial die into 8mm fuel pellets and deposited into the 350kg onboard bunker.

Step C: Precision Docking & Offloading

When the internal pellet hopper reaches 90% capacity, the drone automatically navigates to the canal-bank transfer station. A laser alignment sensor mates with the mobile hopper, discharging dry pellets via an electric auger in 4 minutes.

Step D: Post-Shift Maintenance Flush

Perform a 5-minute high-pressure freshwater wash of the cutter bar and screw press. Inspect propeller Kort nozzles for entangling fishing line or plastic waste. Plug into the canal-side solar charging bank.

9 12-Month Prototype & Field Trial Roadmap

Quarter 1: CAD & Lab Bench Prototyping
  • Hydrodynamic simulation (ANSYS Fluent) of catamaran hull under weed drag.
  • Fabrication of scale screw-press dewaterer; torque and dewatering test bench.
  • Dataset collection and training of YOLOv8 weed-segmentation model.
Quarter 2: Full-Scale Fabrication
  • Welding and CNC fabrication of Al-5083 catamaran hull and conveyor structure.
  • Integration of 48V powertrain, solar canopy, and LiFePO4 battery management.
  • Static buoyancy, balance, and emergency bilge pump validation in tank test.
Quarter 3: Irrigation Canal Field Trials
  • Deployment in a 5 km heavily choked agricultural canal pilot stretch.
  • Validation of automated RTK-GNSS waypoint navigation and collision avoidance.
  • Chemical calorific testing (bomb calorimeter) of extruded biofuel pellets.
Quarter 4: Autonomous Fleet Swarm & Handover
  • Implementation of multi-drone swarm coordination for broad lake clearing.
  • Creation of open-source maintenance manual and training for rural youth cooperatives.
  • Formal presentation to National Irrigation and Water Resources Ministries.
