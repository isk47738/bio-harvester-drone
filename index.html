Problem vs. Innovation

The National Crisis: Choked Canals & Monsoon Floods

Millions of kilometers of irrigation arteries are suffocated by Water Hyacinth (Eichhornia crassipes), causing downstream agrarian drought and deadly urban overflow.

🚨 Conventional Excavators (JCBs)

  • Canal Bank Destruction: Heavy diesel earthmovers fracture fragile earthen linings, accelerating embankment collapse.
  • Excessive Diesel Burn: Consumes ~14 Liters/hour of diesel ($18/hour), emitting heavy greenhouse gases.
  • Leaves Submerged Roots: Merely rips surface foliage, leaving root crowns intact to double in biomass in 12 days.
  • Rotting Roadside Dumps: Piles decomposing weed along roadsides, emitting foul methane gas and breeding disease vectors.

💡 The Jal-Shodhak Breakthrough

  • Zero Embankment Impact: Amphibious ultralight catamaran ($0.22\text{ m}$ draft) navigates directly inside the canal water.
  • Solar-Electric Powered: Rooftop bifacial solar PV + 48V LiFePO4 batteries reduce operational energy cost to $0.40/hour.
  • Sub-Surface Root Severing: Reciprocating tungsten cutters slice roots $0.9\text{ m}$ deep, permanently eradicating weed mats.
  • In-Situ Biofuel Pellets: Squeezes out 85% water and extrudes dry 8mm fuel pellets for rural smokeless cookstoves and kilns.
Circular Engineering

The 4-Stage In-Situ Conversion Pipeline

Turning noxious aquatic biomass into clean rural fuel right inside the waterway.

1

Intake & Cutting

Dual counter-oscillating tungsten blades slice weeds 0.9m sub-surface. Low-torque agitating impellers sweep roots into the conveyor table.

2

Shredder & Dewater

Counter-rotating hexagonal shafts mulch vegetation into 15mm pulp. A continuous progressive screw press expresses 85% water back into the stream.

3

Pelletizer Die

Engine cooling jacket heat (85°C) activates natural lignin binders. A radial roller die compresses the fibers into 8mm dense solid biofuel pellets.

4

Rural Utilization

Pellets with 16.8 MJ/kg calorific value are offloaded via auger to rural cottage kilns and smokeless cookstoves, generating ~$144/day in green revenue.

Engineered for rugged, GPS-denied, long-duration autonomous canal patrols.

Catamaran Hull & Crawlers

Marine-grade Aluminum 5083-H116 with rotomolded HDPE floats. Features retractable rubber paddle tracks to crawl over weirs and bunds.

Perception & Edge AI

NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano (40 TOPS) running YOLOv8 weed-segmentation models, fused with 50m solid-state LiDAR and IP68 stereo depth cameras.

Centimeter RTK-GNSS

Dual-antenna RTK receiver paired with a canal-bank LoRa base station provides ±2 cm waypoint accuracy in narrow 4m-wide canal corridors.

Operational Playbook

Standard Operating Procedure (Field Deployment)

Interactive step-by-step checklist and commands to commission and run the vessel.

Pre-Float Physical Safety Verification

  • Hull Watertightness: Confirm all pontoon inspection hatch seals and IP68 cable glands are secure. Verify bilge sensor indicates 0.0% moisture.
  • Battery Voltage: Ensure 48V LiFePO4 bank is above 51.2V (>90% State of Charge) via the BMS telemetry dashboard.
  • Cutter Clearance: Inspect reciprocating tungsten-carbide teeth gap (<0.4mm) and grease drive eccentrics.
  • Emergency Kill-Switch: Test remote 433MHz emergency RF cutoff beacon (<100ms thruster disengage response).

NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano Software Launch

BASH TERMINAL - JETSON ORIN NANO
# 1. Start core ROS2 daemon and CAN-bus motor drivers
sudo systemctl start jalshodhak-marine-core.service

# 2. Launch LiDAR and IP68 stereo depth camera fusion
ros2 launch perception stereo_lidar_fusion.launch.py

# 3. Load TensorRT-accelerated weed segmentation model (40 TOPS)
ros2 run weed_detector yolov8_waterweed_tensorrt

# 4. Open MAVLink bridge to the STM32 ArduPilot marine autopilot
mavproxy.py --master=/dev/ttyTHS1 --baudrate 921600

QGroundControl Waypoint Configuration

In QGroundControl or Mission Planner, define the canal geofence polygon:

Survey Parameters:

• Lane Overlap: 1.4 meters
• Patrol Speed: 1.8 knots (0.9 m/s)
• Cutter Depth: 0.7 meters sub-surface
• Obstacle Repulsion Margin: 0.8m from canal banks

Failsafe Action:

If RTK lock is lost or current surge exceeds 45A on cutter bar, drone initiates reverse thrusters, raises cutter bar 0.3m, and alerts base station over LoRa.

Automated Pellet Offloading Cycle

When internal pellet bunker reaches 90% capacity (approx 315 kg), the drone autonomously returns to the bank-side transfer station:

  • Laser docking sensor aligns with receiving trailer hopper within ±15mm.
  • High-speed electric auger discharges dry biofuel pellets in under 4 minutes.
  • Vessel resumes scheduled grid harvesting pattern or plugs into solar trickle charging.
